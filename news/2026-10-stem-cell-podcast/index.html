Media Stem Cell Podcast: early-career scientists, live from Seattle

Click to enlarge

Ariel and Dr. Crystal Rogers (UC Davis) were interviewed by hosts Arun Sharma and Daylon James on the Stem Cell Podcast about the challenges and career transitions faced by early-career scientists.

Listen to the episode →

Ariel was invited, together with Dr. Crystal Rogers (UC Davis), to be interviewed by hosts Arun Sharma and Daylon James on the Stem Cell Podcast (Episode 332). The episode was recorded live at the Stem Cells and Developmental Biology Early Career Symposium at the Allen Institute in Seattle, Washington.

Here is the episode summary from the podcast’s page:

In this special episode recorded live in Seattle, Washington at the Allen Institute for the Early Career Development Symposium. Dr. Crystal Rogers, Associate Professor at the UC Davis School of Veterinary Medicine, and Dr. Ariel Waisman, CONICET Investigator at FLENI in Argentina, explore the unique career transitions and systemic challenges faced by early-career scientists. They discuss overcoming imposter syndrome, navigating funding cuts, and adapting to new leadership responsibilities. Emphasizing the importance of strong mentorship, scientific communication, and community, they highlight how peer support and resilience are essential for sustaining scientific progress in today’s economic landscape.
